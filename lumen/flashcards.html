A better way to review

Three small habits
Answer before you flip.
Even a wrong guess makes the correct answer stick better than rereading.
Let “Again” mean again.
Cards you mark again come back sooner. That is the system working.
Keep sessions short.
Ten focused minutes beats an hour of flipping without thinking.

Create a deck

Two columns, one card per line. Keep the front short and the back clear.

One card per line, separated by a vertical bar. You can add more later by making a new deck.
